How It Works

1v1: No dealer. You play directly against your opponent.
Goal: Get as close to 21 as possible without going over.
Turns: Player 1 hits/stands first, then Player 2.
Cards: 2-10 = face value. J/Q/K = 10. A = 1 or 11.
Blackjack: Natural 21 (A + 10-card) beats a regular 21.
Winner: Highest score ≤ 21 wins. Both bust = draw.
Draw: Same score = bets returned to both players.
Forfeit: Leave mid-game and your opponent wins automatically.
