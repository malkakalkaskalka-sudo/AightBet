Rules

Deck: 36 cards (6–A in four suits). One suit is trump.
Goal: Get rid of all your cards. The last player with cards is the "durak" (fool).
Attack: Play a card to attack. Additional attacks must match a rank already on the table.
Defend: Beat each attack card with a higher card of the same suit, or any trump card (if attack isn't trump).
Take: If you can't or won't defend, take all cards from the table into your hand.
Done: Once all attacks are defended, press Done to clear the table and draw back to 6.
Drawing: After each bout both players draw from the deck back up to 6. Attacker draws first.
Betting: Win = 2× your bet. Draw (both empty) = bet returned.
